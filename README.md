# tah-game

**Dead Fields** — an agriculture-themed Dead Cells riff. A single hand-built farm level: run, jump (double-jump included), dodge-roll, and fight your way through crows and boars (feathers and bacon on death) to reach the barn — guarded by a scarecrow boss that spawns waves of crows and won't let you in while it's alive. Collect corn for points; gather enough and you transform into a combine harvester that can't jump but plows straight through regular enemies on contact, drives right through water, and deploys bridges over the remaining pits (the scarecrow can still hurt it, though). Bright Terraria-style low-res canvas (320x180, scaled up with pixelated rendering), procedural Web Audio music/SFX, no asset files, no build step.

Play: https://thataaronguy-sunstream.github.io/tah-game/

**Controls:** `A`/`D` move, `W` jump (works twice in the air), `Space` attack (2-hit combo), `Shift` dodge-roll (i-frames).
